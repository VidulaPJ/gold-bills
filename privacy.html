Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · applies to the Gold Bills apps for Android and Windows

Gold Bills is an offline app for recording and checking gold purchase bills. It is made by Vidula. In short: the app collects nothing, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

What the app stores

The bills you enter or scan (shop, date, weights, rates, charges, tax, totals, HUID, notes), photos or PDFs of bills you add, the gold rates you type in, and your settings.

Where it is stored

Only on your device, encrypted with AES-256. On Android the key is held by the phone's secure keystore and, if you set one, protected by your PIN; your data is excluded from Google's cloud backup. On Windows the key is protected by your Windows account or by a password you choose.

No internet

The Android app has no internet permission, so Android blocks it from connecting to any network. The Windows app makes no network connections. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer, to Google, to Microsoft or to anyone else. Reading bills (text recognition) runs on your device.

No data collected or shared

There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reports or tracking of any kind.

Camera

Used only when you choose to photograph a bill. The photo is saved, encrypted, inside the app.

Files you export

If you export a CSV file or an encrypted backup, the file goes where you choose to save it. CSV files are not encrypted — the app warns you before saving one. Encrypted backups are locked with a password you choose.

Deleting your data

Delete individual bills or uploads in the app, start over from Settings, or uninstall the app. Because everything is on your device, nothing remains with the developer.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers of its own. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
