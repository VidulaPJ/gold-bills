What it does

Made for the way gold is actually bought — bills in several languages, several items per bill, old gold exchanged.

Photograph a bill

Take a photo or pick an image or PDF. The app reads it on your device — English, Hindi, Arabic and more.

Checks every bill

Recalculates gold value, making or wastage, stone and hallmarking charges and tax, and flags totals that don't add up or rates above today's.

Several items, two pages

A bill with many pieces becomes one entry per item, each with its own weight, HUID and making. Two-page bills are joined.

Works anywhere

Pick your country for its currency, tax name, usual rate and number style — or choose Other and set your own. 24K down to 9K, including 23K, 20K and 10K. Extra help for Indian bills: GST split, lakhs, HUID, old-gold exchange.

See what you own

Totals by item, bill, shop, karat and month: cost with and without tax, cost per gram of pure gold, value at today's rate.

Zakat on gold

Pure gold from each bill, gold or silver nisab, worn jewellery in or out — your choice — and the date your gold first reached the nisab.

Private by design

  • No internet permission. Android itself blocks the app from any network connection. You can check: Settings → Apps → Gold Bills → Permissions shows no internet access.
  • Encrypted on your device with AES-256, with an optional PIN (phone) or password (Windows).
  • No account, no ads, no analytics. Nothing about you or your gold is sent anywhere.
  • Backups you control: password-protected backup files that work on both the phone and Windows apps.

Why isn't it on Google Play?
Google requires new developers to run a 14-day test with 12 testers before an app can be listed. Until then, the app is shared here directly. Each file is signed by the developer and listed with a checksum, so you can confirm it hasn't been changed.

Install

Two minutes, once. Updates: download the new version from this page and install it over the old one — your bills stay.

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded GoldBills.apk.
  3. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps.
  4. If Google Play Protect asks to scan the app, let it — then tap Install.
  5. Open Gold Bills and set a PIN.

Windows

  1. Click Download for Windows above.
  2. Right-click the zip → Extract All.
  3. Open GoldBillAnalyzer.exe.
  4. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (the app isn't from the Microsoft Store).
  5. Choose a password, or keep the data locked to your Windows account.

Check your download (optional): compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt. All versions and release notes: releases.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. If you'd like to help, see Support.

What if I lose or change my phone?

Make an encrypted backup in Settings and keep the file somewhere safe (a USB stick, your own drive). Restore it on the new phone or on the Windows app. Because the app never goes online, there is no cloud copy.

Can it check that my jewellery is real or the HUID genuine?

No app can check purity. Gold Bills checks the HUID's format and that the bill's numbers add up; verify each HUID in the BIS CARE app.

Is the zakat calculation a ruling?

No — it's a calculator. Schools and scholars differ on worn jewellery and on the nisab, so both are settings. Follow the view you rely on.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. Choose your country in Settings to set the currency, the tax name (VAT, GST, sales tax…) and how numbers look. Where the tax rate depends on the state or the product — the US, Canada, Europe and others — type the rate shown on your bill once. If your country isn't listed, pick Other and set your own. Gold is weighed in grams.

Which bills does it read?

Clear printed bills, photographed flat and straight on. It has been tuned most on Indian and Gulf jewellers' bills; bills from elsewhere can need a few more corrections, and you can always type a bill in. Always compare what was read with the bill before saving.

Something is wrong or missing

Use the feedback form, or tap Send feedback in the app's Settings. If a bill read wrongly, tap Read wrong? Tell us on the Check bill screen: the form opens with the text the app read already filled in, for you to look over and submit. The app itself never sends anything. You can also report it on GitHub.
