# Gold Bills

Free, offline apps for Android and Windows that keep a private record of the gold you buy, check that every bill adds up
(VAT, GST or sales tax, in any country), show what you own, and work out zakat.

**Download:** https://vidulapj.github.io/gold-bills/

- No internet permission, no account, no ads, no tracking — everything stays on your device, encrypted.
- Each release lists SHA-256 checksums in `SHA256SUMS.txt` so you can check your download.
- Privacy policy: [privacy.html](privacy.html)

Problems or ideas: [open an issue](https://github.com/VidulaPJ/gold-bills/issues).
