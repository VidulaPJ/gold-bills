First public release of **Gold Bills** for Android and Windows.

**Download from the page:** https://vidulapj.github.io/gold-bills/

- **GoldBills.apk** — Android 8 or newer. Open it on your phone and allow installing when asked.
- **GoldBillAnalyzer-Windows.zip** — Windows 10/11. Extract, then open GoldBillAnalyzer.exe ("More info → Run anyway" if Windows warns).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** — checksums to confirm your download.

Includes: scan or type gold bills (English, Hindi, Arabic and more), multi-item and two-page bills, checks for gold value, making and GST/VAT, India mode (₹ lakh amounts, GST 3% or 3% + 5% on making, HUID, hallmarking, old-gold exchange), analysis, zakat on gold, encrypted storage and backups. No internet permission, no account, no ads.
