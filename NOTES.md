## Gold Bills 2.4.4

- **Send feedback** (Android: Settings → Feedback; Windows: Settings → Security & data). Opens your own email app with a message to the developer.
- **Read wrong? Tell us** on the Check bill screen: fills in an email with the text the app read from the bill and what it filled in, so reading problems can be fixed. Delete any names, phone numbers or addresses before sending.
- The app still has no internet permission and never sends anything itself: nothing leaves your device unless you press send in your email app.

Also in 2.4.3: each item is checked against the rate printed on its bill.
