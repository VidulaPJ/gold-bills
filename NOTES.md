## Gold Bills 2.5.0

New checks for the ways jewellery bills really overcharge — not just arithmetic:

- **Stones charged as gold:** an item with stones (ruby, diamond, kundan, polki…) whose gross weight equals its net weight — no stone weight was taken off, so the stones may be charged at the gold rate.
- **GST on making above 3% (India):** for jewellery bought from a jeweller, GST is 3% of the whole bill, making included (GST Council FAQ). The app shows how much more a higher rate on making costs.
- **Wastage / VA:** read as its own charge and shown as a % of the gold value; flagged when it is charged on top of making charges, and counted in the making limit.
- **Purity reminders:** diamond jewellery billed as 22K (it is usually 18K), and an 18K item with stones but no HUID — check the HUID in the BIS Care app.
- Better reading of single-item bills that print gross and net weight side by side.

Android and Windows. Install over the old version — your bills stay.
