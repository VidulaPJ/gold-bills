Gold hallmark and HUID: what to check

Updated 24 November 2026

In short: hallmarked gold jewellery sold in India carries three marks: the BIS logo, the purity (for example 22K916) and a 6-character HUID unique to that piece. Check the HUID on the jewellery matches the bill, then verify it in the official BIS Care app with “Verify HUID”.

The three marks

MarkWhat it tells you
BIS logoTested and hallmarked under the Bureau of Indian Standards scheme.
Purity, e.g. 22K916Carat and fineness: how much of the metal is gold.
HUID, e.g. A1B2C3A 6-character code unique to this piece, recorded by BIS.

Since 1 April 2023, jewellers may only sell hallmarked gold jewellery that carries the 6-character HUID. Older hallmarked jewellery you already own stays valid.

Reading the purity mark

MarkGold content
24K999 / 24K99599.9% / 99.5%

18K75075.0%
14K58558.5%

The bill should show the same purity as the mark, and charge the rate for that purity.

Verify the HUID in BIS Care

  1. Install BIS Care (the official app of the Bureau of Indian Standards) from your app store.
  2. Choose Verify HUID and type the 6 characters stamped on the jewellery. You may need a magnifier: the marks are tiny.
  3. Check that the item type and purity shown match what you bought.

Source: BIS hallmarking FAQ.

On your bill

Good bills list the HUID next to each item. If it's missing, ask for it to be added: it links the bill to that exact piece of jewellery.
